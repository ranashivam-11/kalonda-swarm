## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

First public version: firmware core and ESP32 port, firmware-in-the-loop
campaign, analysis stack, generator-physics companion, documentation.

### Negotiation statistics: from naive to calibrated

Development runs of the SIL showed that the first design's decisions were
driven by noise. Each step below was kept because an experiment showed it
helped, and the ablation in campaign experiment E5 reproduces the starting
point.

1. **Sign-tracked steering.** Negotiating a fixed signed offset gave about
   zero expected gain under ±4° direction wander, because half the time
   the wake was pushed onto the neighbour. The node now learns only a
   magnitude. The sign follows the side of the nearest downstream rotor,
   with 0.1 D hysteresis.
2. **Pooled sequential test** (`KS_OUT_HOLD`, trial log record version
   2). A one-shot "accept if z > 1" rule let *selfish* nodes random-walk to
   16–23° offsets. Repeats of the same contrast are now pooled by inverse
   variance until z > 2 (accept), z < −1 (reject), or the pool is full.
3. **Null calibration of the standard error** (`kappa_prior`,
   `kappa_memory`, `NULL_CAL` events). Even pooled, the block-based standard
   error was too small by about 2–2.7×: the z-scores of selfish trials had
   standard deviation 2.4, and 37 % exceeded |z| = 2. Nodes now measure
   their own null distribution in rounds in which they take no part.
   Firmware test: calibrated null decisions have a z standard deviation of
   1.07.
4. **DRIFT made informational; participants check their own offset**
   (`KS_Q_SELF`). Aborting on A1/A2 disagreement discarded 18 % of
   downstream reports, exactly from the waked rotors where steering
   matters. The merged-A variance already down-weights such trials.
   Cooperative trial aborts fell from 37 % to 22 % at that stage (12.7 %
   with the final defaults, campaign E1).
5. **Timing and steps.** Defaults are now 30-s phases with 9 s settle and
   3-s blocks, first steps of 10°, and pools of up to 12 trials. In paired
   36-h night runs, cooperative energy rose from 5 730 Wh (60-s phases, 6°
   steps, pool 6) to 5 884 Wh. Short phases reject slow wind wander more
   strongly in the A–B–B–A contrast.

### Analysis

* **The per-step ledger is biased by selection (winner's curse).** Steps
  are accepted when their measured score is high, so the own and
  downstream changes booked on accepted steps over-state the gains. The
  README's "who gave, who received" figure therefore compares each unit
  against greedy held fixed on the same wind. In the field, the same
  comparison comes from toggled hours.
* **Campaign.** The results in the README come from the 1.0.0 firmware
  defaults above: 16 runs, 1 608 simulated hours, regenerated by
  `scripts/run_sil_campaign.py` and summarised by
  `scripts/summarise_campaign.py`, which prints the README's results table
  verbatim.

### Model fixes found by the test suite

* **Near-wake deficit.** For x < x₀ the deficit used to ramp up from zero
  at the rotor. The near wake now follows FLORIS: the width blends from
  0.501 D √(Ct/2) to σ₀, and the deficit decays monotonically from about
  0.93. The campaign grid (5 D) lies in the far wake, so its results were
  barely affected.
* **Campaign output names.** Experiments such as `E2_beta_0.25` contain
  dots, and `Path.with_suffix` had truncated them, so runs overwrote each
  other. Files are now named explicitly.
* **Picklable diurnal profile.** The 72-h diurnal experiments could not
  be sent to worker processes. The profile is now a dataclass rather than
  a closure.
* **Discrete-gradient test with cogging.** A 10 ms step does not resolve
  the 48 detents per revolution, and Newton stalls. The energy-balance test
  now runs a smooth rotor at 10 ms, and a cogging rotor at 0.2 ms with the
  detents resolved.
